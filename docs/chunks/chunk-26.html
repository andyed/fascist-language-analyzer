Theme: 13. Selective Populism

"Progressive elites speak in lofty terms of openness, progress, expertise, cooperation, and globalization. But too often, these terms are just rhetorical Trojan horses concealing their true intention—stripping “we the people” of our constitutional authority over our country’s future."

The text frames the 'People' as a monolithic entity whose authority is being stolen by a specific, illegitimate 'elite' class. It treats 'expertise' and 'cooperation' not as valid concepts but as deceptive tools ('Trojan horses') used by an enemy of the 'People.' This aligns with Eco's description of the leader/movement interpreting the 'Common Will' against 'delegated' or 'expert' institutions.
