"Ignoring these two challenges will simply continue the parasitic draining of the American manufacturing and defense industrial base."

The use of the term 'parasitic draining' to describe economic trade relations is a hallmark of Newspeak. It replaces complex economic concepts (like trade deficits, comparative advantage, or supply chain shifts) with a biological/medical metaphor that characterizes external entities as organisms feeding off a host. This type of highly charged, simplified vocabulary is designed to foreclose critical reasoning and frame policy issues as existential threats to the national 'body.'
