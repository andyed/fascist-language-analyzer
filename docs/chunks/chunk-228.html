While framed as 'neutrality,' this passage demands that the Intelligence Community (IC) subordinate its independent assessment and expertise to the 'decision-making authority' of the President. Eco notes that in Ur-Fascism, 'the critical spirit makes distinctions' and 'to distinguish is a sign of modernism.' This proposal to fire and strip benefits from employees based on 'suspicion' of leaking—rather than proven criminal activity—and the framing of such disclosures as a 'weapon in policy debates' characterizes internal dissent or the release of information as an act of betrayal (treason) rather than a potential act of whistleblowing or a legal/procedural violation.
