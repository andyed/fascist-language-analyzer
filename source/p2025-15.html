Reform the Negotiated Rulemaking Process at ED The U.S. Department of Education is required by statute 14 to engage in nego - tiated rulemaking prior to promulgating new regulations under Subchapter 1 of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act as well as Subchapters II (Teacher Quality Enhancements) and IV of the Higher Education Act of 1964 (Student Assistance).
