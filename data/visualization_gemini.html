Pacifism is Trafficking with the Enemy (Conf: 0.8)
"For conservatives to have a fighting chance to take on the Administrative State and reform our federal government, the work must start now."

The text frames political administration and reform not as a process of deliberation or democratic compromise, but as a 'fight' against an internal adversary (the 'Administrative State'). "To execute requires a well-conceived, coordinated, unified plan and a trained and committed cadre of personnel to implement it."

The emphasis on a 'trained and committed cadre' and the urgency of 'execution' suggests a preference for disciplined, ideological action over critical reflection or pluralistic debate. The term 'cadre' often implies a militant or highly disciplined ideological unit.

Cult of Tradition (Conf: 0.6)
"With this volume, we have gone back to the future—and then some."

This phrase suggests that the solutions for the future are found by looking backward to a perceived golden age of 'revolution' (the 1980 Reagan era). It implies that the fundamental truths of governance are already known and must be recovered.

Selective Populism (Conf: 0.6)
"The 2025 Presidential Transition Project is the conservative movement’s unified effort to be ready for the next conservative Administration..."

The text presents the 'conservative movement' as a monolithic entity with a 'unified effort.' This mirrors the concept of Selective Populism where 'The People' (or a specific subset thereof) is conceived as a monolithic entity expressing a single Common Will.
